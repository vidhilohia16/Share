Workflow & Decision Engine Specification

Step 7 Recruitment Note & Architecture Trade-Offs

Target Persona: The Outdoor Sightseer & Leisure Explorer

We explicitly committed to advising the leisure traveler walking through urban landmarks, visiting outdoor markets, and dining al-fresco.

  • Why this persona? Raw forecasts state numbers like "34°C max, 7.2 UV". To a sightseer, that means "Hot & intense sun — explore early morning or after 4 PM, wear SPF 50".
  • Focus metrics: Apparent (feels-like) temperature, UV Index, precipitation sum/probability, and wind gusts.

Decision Rules & Threshold Matrix

Metric Signal Threshold Condition Verdict Output
Extreme Weather / Storm WMO Code ≥ 80 or Wind > 45 km/h "Weather alert: Heavy rain/strong winds expected — stay indoors or keep plans flexible."
High Rain Risk Precip Prob > 60% OR Precip Sum > 5.0 mm "Rain likely — carry an umbrella/raincoat and plan indoor activities for peak hours."
Intense Heat & UV Max Temp > 32°C OR UV Index > 8.0 "Hot & intense sun — explore early morning or after 4 PM, wear SPF 50."
Chilly & Wind Min Temp < 12°C OR Wind > 25 km/h "Chilly & breezy — layer up with a windbreaker if walking outdoors."
Unremarkable Day Mild (18-28°C), Rain < 20%, UV < 6, Wind < 20km/h "Pleasant & comfortable — great day for strolling, sightseeing, and outdoor dining."
Multi-Condition Prioritization Rule

When multiple signals trigger simultaneously (e.g. 35°C AND 70% Rain), the decision engine prioritizes Safety/Rain > Extreme Heat > Cold/Wind > Mild to ensure the primary plain-language sentence is actionable and unambiguous.

One Thing Deliberately Left Out: Hourly Interactive Charts

We deliberately omitted detailed 24-hour line graphs and hourly slider controls.

Rationale: Travel planning is ruined by cognitive overload. If a user has to read through hourly temperature curves to decide whether to bring an umbrella, our product failed its core promise. We condensed hourly metrics directly into actionable plain language time blocks (e.g. "explore before 11 AM or after 4 PM").

What We Would Build Next (v2 Roadmap)

  1. Interactive Activity Feasibility Scores: Ratings for specific activities like "Outdoor Photography (9/10)", "Hiking/Trekking (4/10 due to afternoon heat)", or "Rooftop Dining (8/10)".
  2. Itinerary Rain Radar Alerts: PWA push notifications that alert travelers 30 minutes before sudden local rain showers hit their current GPS location.
  3. Trip Luggage Weight Budget Calculator: Estimates total bag weight based on clothing item recommendations (e.g. 3 jackets vs 5 t-shirts).
